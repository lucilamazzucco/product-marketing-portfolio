01 / Consumer insight

What we know

FCA research reports high awareness of cryptoassets among UK adults, but awareness is not the same as ownership, intent or understanding. The research does not tell us how Revolut customers behave.

02 / Consumer insight

Priority audience

Existing, eligible UK Revolut customers who are curious about crypto but have limited hands-on experience. This is a proposed behavioral segment, not a validated customer persona.

03 / Consumer insight

Customer situation to investigate

A customer opens the Crypto area to learn what is available, compare costs and risks, and decide whether to explore further. We need to establish how common this situation is and whether existing information already answers their questions.

04 / Consumer insight

Research priorities

Interview crypto-aware non-users and occasional users. Investigate what prompted their interest, how they currently research crypto, which alternatives they consider, and what information they need before deciding. Test understanding of risk, costs and restrictions without encouraging trading.

05 / Consumer insight

Decision for the business

Do not allocate a full launch budget until the segment's size, needs, suitability and potential value are supported by customer research and product data.
