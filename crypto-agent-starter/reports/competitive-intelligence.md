## Strategic opportunities — prioritized hypotheses

### 1. Make eligibility, fees and risk easier to understand

**Rationale:** Customers may need clearer explanations before deciding whether crypto is appropriate for them. This is a hypothesis, not measured friction.

**Experiment:** Run UK moderated comprehension sessions comparing current public messaging with a proposed plain-language guide. **Measure:** accurate recall of risks, eligibility and total costs; avoid using trading conversion alone as success.

### 2. Test the integrated-app positioning against specialist exchanges

**Rationale:** Revolut's broader finance context and Coinbase's specialist proposition may influence consideration differently. The preference is unknown.

**Experiment:** Blind message testing with crypto-curious UK participants; compare clarity, relevance and perceived credibility. **Measure:** correct understanding, message recall and stated consideration, with risk comprehension guardrails.

### 3. Make educational content match decision-stage questions

**Rationale:** A structured education journey might reduce uncertainty, but there is no behavioral evidence yet.

**Experiment:** Prototype stage-specific FAQs and compare task completion and comprehension in usability testing. **Measure:** ability to locate answers and accurately explain relevant risks and fees.

## Recommended next decision

**Start with opportunity #1** because it is researchable with public information and a small participant study, has a clear customer-comprehension outcome, and does not depend on access to Revolut's internal analytics. Treat this as a proposed research priority, not a proven commercial uplift.
