Crypto Discovery Journey.

Connecting product discovery, existing education and responsible lifecycle engagement for crypto-curious customers.

Product MarketingExperience StrategyLifecycle CRMConcept / Not live
THE OBSERVATION

Revolut already presents market data, Learn resources, Strategies and Earn features in the experience shown in user-provided screenshots.

THE HYPOTHESIS

A more connected, self-directed path between these surfaces may improve orientation and informed understanding. This is not a verified customer problem.

03 / Customer journey

From exploration to understanding.

01 / DISCOVER

Choose a goal

Start with the customer's question rather than a trading CTA.

02 / LEARN

Use existing resources

Surface relevant educational material at feature level.

03 / EVALUATE

Understand the trade-offs

Make costs, limitations and risks easy to find.

04 / DECIDE

Leave the choice open

Allow users to continue learning or stop without pressure.

04 / Lifecycle activation

Support the journey, not the transaction.

IN-APP CONTENT CARD

First educational exploration

Audience: eligible users who choose to explore educational material.

“New to crypto? Start with how it works, what it costs and what risks to consider.”
Measure: content discovery and correct understanding.
CONTEXTUAL MESSAGE

Feature information request

Trigger: a user explicitly opens a feature explainer.

“Exploring staking? Learn about variable rewards, restrictions and risks first.”
Measure: comprehension, not feature conversion.
OPT-IN EDUCATIONAL EMAIL

Continue learning

Trigger: an explicit preference for educational follow-up, subject to consent.

“Continue learning: a closer look at crypto costs and risks.”
Guardrails: opt-out, frequency cap, no trading prompts from learning completion.
05 / Validation plan

What would make this worth building?

DAYS 1–30

Verify the experience

Map the actual UK journey, confirm product availability and disclosures, conduct discovery interviews.

DAYS 31–60

Test the concept

Compare current explanations with prototypes; measure comprehension, navigation success and false confidence.

DAYS 61–90

Make the decision

Only propose an approved pilot if customer evidence and Legal/Compliance review support it.

Primary success criterion: informed understanding

Can eligible UK adults accurately explain relevant costs, material risks and feature restrictions? Reject a concept that increases confidence while weakening risk comprehension. No user testing or performance uplift is claimed.
