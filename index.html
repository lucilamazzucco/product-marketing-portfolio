Your First
Bitcoin Decision.

From curiosity to informed adoption. A focused go-to-market proposal for existing Revolut customers who explore Bitcoin but have never purchased crypto.

Customer-led positioningLifecycle activationExperiment design

Independent speculative concept, not affiliated with Revolut. UK product screens, CRM and analytics have not been accessed. All campaign screens below are proposed, not live Revolut interfaces.

01 / The opportunity

Interest is a signal.
Not an explanation.

Imagine an existing Revolut customer who views Bitcoin more than once but has never bought crypto. We know they are exploring. We do not know whether they need clarity on price movements, transaction costs, protections, or simply more time.

AUDIENCE HYPOTHESIS

Curious, not yet invested

Eligible UK customers with repeated Bitcoin information views and no previous crypto purchase. Audience threshold and size require internal validation.

REAL ALTERNATIVES

Keep browsing, search elsewhere, or walk away

Our concept must offer more relevant guidance than another generic prompt to buy.

RESEARCH SIGNAL

Different reasons to hesitate

FCA UK research highlights understanding, risk and lack of protections among reasons some non-owners do not plan to buy crypto. This does not establish the barriers of Revolut Bitcoin browsers.

02 / The idea

Don't guess what the customer needs.
Ask.

One optional in-app prompt, triggered on a later eligible visit, lets customers choose what they want to understand. Their answer routes them to relevant information and existing Revolut capabilities. A richer simulation is proposed as a separately validated product opportunity.

03 / PMM strategy & GTM

Position the experience around a decision, not a trade.

POSITIONING

Explore what matters before your first Bitcoin decision.

For curious existing Revolut customers, a guided starting point to understand possible outcomes, costs, risks and alternatives to buying.

MVP

Launch with what exists

Test the question-led entry point and route to verified UK Crypto Learn, fee/risk information and watchlist or alerts. No new trading functionality required for the first test.

PRODUCT PARTNERSHIP

Prototype one new experience

Explore an interactive £ amount and time-range simulation, showing both positive and negative historical outcomes and hypothetical scenarios. Validate usefulness, fairness and compliance before build.

Creative principle

Do not promise easy returns or imply that past performance predicts future results. A customer choosing not to invest can be a successful informed outcome.

04 / Experiment & decision

Does customer-led guidance improve informed adoption?

Randomise eligible repeat Bitcoin explorers into a holdout (current experience) and a test group (question-led entry point). Confirm segmentation, current UK journeys and any overlapping campaigns with Product, Data and CRM before launch.

PRIMARY EXPERIMENT SIGNAL

Meaningful next steps

Incremental qualified engagement with relevant Crypto information or exploration features versus holdout, not just prompt clicks.

BUSINESS OUTCOMES

Informed first adoption

Measure eligible first purchases and subsequent use as secondary outcomes, with proper attribution and sufficient observation time. No assumed uplift.

CUSTOMER GUARDRAILS

Understanding over urgency

Review comprehension, complaints, unwanted prompts, dismissal and misleading expectations. Compliance has approval authority over copy, targeting and flows.

Decision rule: scale only if the experience improves meaningful engagement without worsening customer-understanding or conduct signals; otherwise iterate or stop. Research: UK consumer evidence · Competitive review · Swiss-account product observations. Official UK feature eligibility and fee details require rechecking at implementation.
