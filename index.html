From curiosity to informed adoption. A focused go-to-market proposal for existing Revolut customers who explore Bitcoin but have never purchased crypto.

Customer-led positioningLifecycle activationExperiment design

Independent speculative concept, not affiliated with Revolut. UK product screens, CRM and analytics have not been accessed. All campaign screens below are proposed, not live Revolut interfaces.

01 / The opportunity

Interest is a signal.
Not an explanation.

Imagine an existing Revolut customer who views Bitcoin more than once but has never bought crypto. We know they are exploring. We do not know whether they need clarity on price movements, transaction costs, protections, or simply more time. The commercial hypothesis: Revolut may have an opportunity to turn existing Bitcoin curiosity into informed first-time crypto adoption by helping customers find the next step that matters to them.

AUDIENCE HYPOTHESIS

Curious, not yet invested

Eligible UK customers with repeated Bitcoin information views and no previous crypto purchase. Audience threshold and size require internal validation.

REAL ALTERNATIVES

Keep browsing, search elsewhere, or walk away

Our concept must offer more relevant guidance than another generic prompt to buy.

RESEARCH SIGNAL

Different reasons to hesitate

FCA UK research highlights understanding, risk and lack of protections among reasons some non-owners do not plan to buy crypto. This does not establish the barriers of Revolut Bitcoin browsers.

02 / The idea

Don't guess what the customer needs.
Ask.

One optional in-app prompt, triggered on a later eligible visit, lets customers choose what they want to understand. Their answer routes them to relevant information and existing Revolut capabilities. The first test uses a question-led message linking to existing Revolut features. The historical explorer below illustrates a possible future product experience, not a live Revolut feature or part of the initial test.

03 / Launch & activation plan

Test the message before building the feature.

Pilot the question-led in-app message with eligible UK Bitcoin explorers. Link to existing Crypto Learn, fee and risk information, and watchlists without building a new feature.

PILOT DECISION

Launch small. Learn before scaling.

Product and Analytics set eligibility and the holdout test. CRM delivers the message; Compliance approves the experience. Review first-purchase uplift and customer guardrails before expanding.

04 / Success measurement

Did we turn curiosity into informed adoption?

A proposed 50/50 randomised test: eligible repeat Bitcoin explorers see either the current experience (control) or the question-led journey (test). Measure outcomes across all assigned customers, not just people who engage with the prompt.

PRIMARY BUSINESS KPI

Incremental first-time crypto adoption

30-day first-purchase rate, test vs control, among eligible assigned customers. Report absolute percentage-point difference and relative uplift, with uncertainty intervals. No assumed result or arbitrary target.

DIAGNOSTIC

Qualified engagement

7-day rate of completing a relevant action after the prompt: exploring fees or risks, starting Crypto Learn, or using watchlist/alerts. Compare test vs control where equivalent events are available.

RETENTION

Repeat crypto usage

30-day return to Crypto features or subsequent eligible crypto activity, assessed separately from the initial purchase.

CUSTOMER GUARDRAIL

Risk comprehension

Check understanding of volatility and limitations of protection, plus complaints, unwanted prompts and signs of pressure. Any deterioration triggers review.

AFTER THE UK PILOT

What would we do with the results?

Expand the in-app journey

If more eligible Bitcoin explorers make a first purchase, with no decline in risk understanding, extend the question-led entry point to a broader eligible audience.

Improve the next step

If customers explore fees, risks or watchlists but do not progress, review which questions they choose and whether existing destinations actually answer them before changing the message.

Pause the prompt

If it increases unwanted nudges, confusion about risk or complaints, stop the test—even if first purchases rise.

Agree the baseline, minimum detectable effect, sample size and test duration with Analytics before launch. The interactive simulator is a separate, conditional phase-two product hypothesis and is not included in this MVP test.
