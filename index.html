Featured project / Independent case study
Revolut Crypto · UK-focused research

Making crypto easier to understand before investing.

The PMM question: How can Revolut help crypto-curious customers explore digital assets, understand the risks and costs, and make informed decisions within an everyday financial app?

Scope: Revolut Crypto in the main Revolut app. Revolut X is considered only where its promotion or disclosure appears in that experience; its advanced trading journey is a separate research opportunity.

Independent portfolio project. Not affiliated with, commissioned or endorsed by Revolut. Observations from one app session are distinguished from hypotheses; no internal customer data or experiment results are claimed.

01 / Customer understanding

A journey designed around informed choice.

Working hypothesis: an existing Revolut customer exploring crypto may need clarity about the product, fees and risks before deciding whether to use it. The journey below is a proposed research framework, not Revolut's internal funnel.

01Discover

Enters the crypto section; may encounter an execution-model disclosure and Revolut X promotion.

02Explore

Checks available assets, price information and features.

03Learn

Seeks plain-language explanations of fees, volatility and protections.

04Decide

Assesses suitability, costs and risks without purchase pressure.

05Optional first use

May choose to transact after appropriate checks and disclosures.

06Ongoing relationship

Needs transparent updates, ongoing education and support.

Observed entry-point experience

In one first-hand session, a technical comparison of the Revolut app and Revolut X appeared immediately upon entering Crypto — before initiating a trade. A Revolut X promotional banner was also noticed within the main Crypto area. These are observations, not proof of a widespread friction point.

Research question: Does this information improve customer understanding, or introduce complexity at the discovery stage?

PMM strategic opportunities

From market intelligence to decisions.

01 / Clearer decision-making

Hypothesis: customers may benefit from simpler explanations of crypto risk, fees and eligibility. Test comprehension with UK participants.

02 / Positioning

Test whether an integrated financial app or a specialist exchange proposition feels more relevant and credible.

03 / Education

Prototype FAQs tailored to discovery, evaluation and onboarding. Measure understanding, not trading activity.

Research hypotheses only. No customer study or measured results are claimed.

03 / First-hand observation

Technical transparency meets customer comprehension.

An entry-point communication explains execution models, fees and risks across Revolut Crypto and Revolut X. The opportunity is to test whether additional plain-language guidance improves comprehension without weakening mandatory disclosures.

WHAT WAS OBSERVED

Technical comparison on entry

  • Displayed on entering the Crypto section, before attempting a trade.
  • Explains OTC execution versus an order-book model.
  • Includes counterparty roles, fee structures and execution risks.
  • Uses specialist terminology that may require further explanation.
PROPOSED PMM RESEARCH

Test a clearer learning layer

  • Retain full mandatory terms, pricing disclosures and risk information.
  • Test an accessible summary followed by complete details, subject to compliance review.
  • Measure understanding of trading models, costs and risks.
  • Check for confusion, misleading impressions and accessibility issues.
