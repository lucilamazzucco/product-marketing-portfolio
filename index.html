01 / The opportunity

Interest is a signal.
Not an explanation.

Imagine an existing Revolut customer who views Bitcoin more than once but has never bought crypto. We know they are exploring. We do not know whether they need clarity on price movements, transaction costs, protections, or simply more time.

AUDIENCE HYPOTHESIS

Curious, not yet invested

Eligible UK customers with repeated Bitcoin information views and no previous crypto purchase. Audience threshold and size require internal validation.

REAL ALTERNATIVES

Keep browsing, search elsewhere, or walk away

Our concept must offer more relevant guidance than another generic prompt to buy.

RESEARCH SIGNAL

Different reasons to hesitate

FCA UK research highlights understanding, risk and lack of protections among reasons some non-owners do not plan to buy crypto. This does not establish the barriers of Revolut Bitcoin browsers.

02 / The idea

Don't guess what the customer needs.
Ask.

One optional in-app prompt, triggered on a later eligible visit, lets customers choose what they want to understand. Their answer routes them to relevant information and existing Revolut capabilities. 03 / Positioning & launch strategy

Lead with the customer's question, not a trade.

The positioning choice is deliberate: repeated Bitcoin views signal curiosity, not readiness. We would test whether a question-led experience is more relevant than a generic education message or a direct trading prompt.

ALTERNATIVE A

“Learn about Bitcoin”

Useful, but generic. It assumes education is what everyone needs and gives little reason to act now.

ALTERNATIVE B

“Start buying Bitcoin”

Directly commercial, but it assumes purchase intent and may be inappropriate for undecided customers.

RECOMMENDED HYPOTHESIS

“Explore what matters before you decide”

Lets customers self-identify their question, then find relevant Revolut information or features. The benefit to validate: a more useful next step without pressure to buy.

Why Revolut?

For existing Revolut customers, the potential advantage is exploring their questions and applicable product information in an app they already use, rather than piecing together generic answers elsewhere. This is a positioning hypothesis, not a proven competitive advantage.

04 / Success measurement

Did we turn curiosity into informed adoption?

A proposed 50/50 randomised test: eligible repeat Bitcoin explorers see either the current experience (control) or the question-led journey (test). Measure outcomes across all assigned customers, not just people who engage with the prompt.

PRIMARY BUSINESS KPI

Incremental first-time crypto adoption

30-day first-purchase rate, test vs control, among eligible assigned customers. Report absolute percentage-point difference and relative uplift, with uncertainty intervals. No assumed result or arbitrary target.

DIAGNOSTIC

Qualified engagement

7-day rate of completing a relevant action after the prompt: exploring fees or risks, starting Crypto Learn, or using watchlist/alerts. Compare test vs control where equivalent events are available.

RETENTION

Repeat crypto usage

30-day return to Crypto features or subsequent eligible crypto activity, assessed separately from the initial purchase.

CUSTOMER GUARDRAIL

Risk comprehension

Check understanding of volatility and limitations of protection, plus complaints, unwanted prompts and signs of pressure. Any deterioration triggers review.

Scale · Iterate · Stop

Scale if incremental first-time adoption improves with adequate statistical evidence and no deterioration in customer guardrails. Iterate if engagement improves but adoption does not, investigating customer value and journey friction. Stop or review if there is no meaningful benefit or if comprehension and conduct worsen, even when purchases increase.

Agree the baseline, minimum detectable effect, sample size and test duration with Analytics before launch. The interactive simulator is a separate, conditional phase-two product hypothesis and is not included in this MVP test.
