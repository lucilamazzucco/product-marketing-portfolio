Revolut Crypto / Independent UK product marketing proposal

Curious about Bitcoin. Not ready to buy.

A targeted product marketing experiment to help existing Revolut customers move from passive Bitcoin browsing to informed product exploration, without treating every visit as purchase intent.

Existing UK featuresBehaviour-led audienceOne focused experiment

Independent concept, not affiliated with Revolut. No access to Revolut UK app analytics or internal CRM. Visuals are proposed communications, not replicas of live UK screens.

01 / Customer and opportunity

The behaviour is real enough to target. The reason behind it is not yet known.

Target hypothesis: an eligible Revolut customer visits Bitcoin information at least twice in seven days, makes no crypto purchase and later returns to the app. They may be learning, evaluating, waiting or simply curious. Our first job is to understand which.

OBSERVED SIGNAL TO TEST

Repeat Bitcoin exploration

Asset-detail views, no purchase. Exact audience size and event instrumentation must be checked with Analytics.

CUSTOMER ALTERNATIVE

Do nothing or learn elsewhere

Revisit the price, search online, compare providers or decide not to invest. Non-purchase is a legitimate outcome.

EXTERNAL CONTEXT

Understanding and risk matter

FCA 2025: among people who neither own nor plan to own crypto, 22% cite lack of understanding, 17% risk and 12% lack of protection as their main reason not to buy. This is not Revolut segment data.

FCA research
02 / Positioning decision

Sell the value of understanding, not the promise of easy investing.

Revolut UK already offers Bitcoin information, Crypto Learn, watchlists and price alerts before crypto onboarding is complete. The differentiated opportunity to test is contextual discovery: connecting a customer's demonstrated interest to the right existing tool at the right time.

FOR

Interested, undecided customers

Existing eligible users exploring Bitcoin but not yet choosing to trade.

RELEVANT ALTERNATIVE

Unstructured browsing

Repeatedly checking prices or looking elsewhere for explanations, without a clear next step.

VALUE PROPOSITION

Explore Bitcoin on your terms

Understand the asset and its risks, or follow its price without buying. Buying is one possible future decision, not the campaign's promised outcome.

Why not round-ups?

Crypto spare change exists in UK, but automatic purchasing solves a different job: convenient repeat buying after a customer has decided they want exposure. It is not an evidence-based answer to uncertainty about Bitcoin. Keep it outside this experiment.

03 / Proposed lifecycle activation

One trigger. One message. A useful choice.

Instead of recreating Revolut's UK screens, these are two proposed communication states within one journey. The card appears on a later appropriate Home visit, subject to eligibility, permissions and frequency limits.

04 / Experiment and decision

Test whether relevance improves informed exploration.

Randomise eligible repeat explorers into a contextual-card group and a holdout with the existing experience. Review copy and placements with UK Compliance before launch.

PRIMARY OUTCOME

Meaningful feature discovery

Incremental Crypto Learn starts or voluntary watchlist/alert exploration relative to holdout, not raw card clicks.

CUSTOMER QUALITY

Understanding and agency

Check risk comprehension, perceived relevance, dismissals and whether customers understand they need not invest.

COMMERCIAL / GUARDRAILS

Informed downstream decisions

Monitor eligible onboarding and purchases as secondary descriptive outcomes, alongside complaints, misleading expectations and unwanted prompts. Never optimise for pressure to trade.

Validate with Product, Data, Research and Compliance: segment size, live UK customer journey, whether similar CRM exists, and which messages are permitted. UK onboarding and available features · UK Crypto Learn · UK spare change · Research appendix · Product observations (Swiss account).
