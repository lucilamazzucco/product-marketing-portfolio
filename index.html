Revolut Crypto / Growth recommendation

Turn crypto curiosity into informed participation.

Revolut already brings crypto features and education into the same financial app. I recommend testing a more connected discovery experience for existing customers who are interested in crypto but have limited experience.

UK marketExisting customersProposed strategy

Independent analysis, not affiliated with Revolut. Customer needs, segment value and commercial impact have not been validated.

01 / Growth opportunity

Existing features are the starting point, not the missing piece.

Revolut's Crypto experience includes market information, Learn, recurring purchases, trading controls and staking. The commercial question is whether interested existing customers can find enough relevant information to decide if any of these products is right for them.

CUSTOMER

Crypto-curious Revolut users

Eligible UK customers with limited hands-on experience. Prioritised as a test audience, not a proven high-value segment.

ALTERNATIVES

Doing nothing is a real option

Customers may postpone a decision, research independently or use a specialist exchange. The right comparison is not limited to other finance apps.

GROWTH HYPOTHESIS

Clarity may support adoption

If customers understand features, costs and risks more easily, more eligible and interested users may make informed decisions. This has not been demonstrated.

Recommended focus

Test whether contextual explanations and clearer feature navigation improve comprehension and qualified product exploration. Do not start by creating new courses or incentivising trading.

02 / Positioning rationale

Revolut's advantage to test is context, not simply convenience.

A familiar financial app can reduce the need to switch between services. But integrated access is not unique, and familiarity must never imply lower investment risk. The stronger proposition to investigate combines existing product access with clear information at the point of decision.

WHY THIS AUDIENCE

Start with a specific customer situation

An existing customer becomes curious about crypto, opens the Crypto area and wants to understand their options before taking any action. We need to verify whether this situation is common, whether the current experience already serves it well and whether these customers are suitable for the product.

WHY REVOLUT

Connect assets the business already has

Market discovery, educational content and crypto features already coexist in the app. The proposed advantage is how they work together, not a claim that Revolut has exclusive educational content or safer crypto products.

03 / Proposed experience

Connect the questions customers ask to the features they explore.

Use existing Learn content at the point of exploration, make costs and restrictions easy to find, and allow customers to leave without pressure to trade.

04 / Activation

Keep communication tied to customer intent.

Educational follow-up should be based on explicit interest and consent, not on an assumption that completing a lesson means someone is ready to invest.

IN-APP CONTENT CARD

First educational exploration

Audience: eligible users who choose to explore educational material.

“New to crypto? Start with how it works, what it costs and what risks to consider.”
Measure: content discovery and correct understanding.
CONTEXTUAL MESSAGE

Feature information request

Trigger: a user explicitly opens a feature explainer.

“Exploring staking? Learn about variable rewards, restrictions and risks first.”
Measure: comprehension, not feature conversion.
OPT-IN EDUCATIONAL EMAIL

Continue learning

Trigger: an explicit preference for educational follow-up, subject to consent.

“Continue learning: a closer look at crypto costs and risks.”
Guardrails: opt-out, frequency cap, no trading prompts from learning completion.
05 / Investment decision

Prove customer value before committing to rollout.

PRIMARY CUSTOMER OUTCOME

Accurate understanding

Can customers explain material risks, fees, restrictions and differences between the relevant features?

COMMERCIAL SIGNAL

Qualified exploration

Among eligible interested customers, track meaningful feature exploration and informed opt-in, not raw transaction volume.

SAFETY GUARDRAIL

No false confidence

Reject changes that improve navigation or engagement while increasing misconceptions about protection, returns or losses.

GO / NO-GO

Evidence first

Check the live UK experience, validate segment size and needs, and test the prototype with Research, Product and Compliance before funding implementation.

06 / Evidence and limitations

What supports the recommendation.

The current recommendation draws on public consumer research, official competitor communications and four user-provided Revolut Crypto screenshots. These sources inform a hypothesis; they cannot establish Revolut customer behaviour or the size of a growth opportunity.

UK CONSUMER CONTEXT

Awareness is not adoption

FCA/YouGov 2025 reported 91% cryptoasset awareness among the UK general public. This does not establish intent, confidence or unmet demand.

FCA research
COMPETITIVE CONTEXT

Integration is not exclusive

Official communications from Revolut, PayPal, bunq and N26 describe access, costs and restrictions in different ways. Geographic availability differs, and the comparison does not establish customer preference.

PRODUCT OBSERVATION

Existing assets are visible

Four screenshots show Learn, Strategies, Earn and market discovery in a Spanish-language, CHF-denominated experience. UK journeys and disclosures require separate verification.

Product review

The crypto-curious audience, the positioning and the proposed screens remain unvalidated. No customer interviews, Revolut analytics, live campaign results or implementation outcomes are claimed.
