02 / Positioning & growth strategy

From crypto curiosity to informed adoption.

Business challenge: How might Revolut increase informed crypto adoption among eligible existing UK customers? This independent recommendation builds on completed public-source desk research. Segment needs, messaging effectiveness and commercial impact still require validation.

TARGET SEGMENT · HYPOTHESIS

Crypto-curious existing customers

Eligible UK adults who already use Revolut but have little or no crypto experience. Prioritized for research because integrated access and comprehension can be tested together—not because segment size or value has been established.

POSITIONING · RECOMMENDED FOR TESTING

Crypto in context

Pair familiar access inside a finance app with transparent, risk-balanced information. Convenience is a category promise; clear decision support is a possible positioning territory, not a proven differentiator.

BUSINESS OUTCOME · CONDITIONAL

Informed adoption, not conversion at any cost

First demonstrate better understanding of fees, restrictions and risk. Only then explore responsible activation, with eligibility, compliance and customer-outcome guardrails.

PRIMARY RESEARCH AUDIENCE · PROTO-PERSONA

The Informed Explorer

Who: an eligible UK adult, aware of crypto but with limited practical experience, who may already use a finance app. Existing Revolut usage is a recruiting criterion to verify, not a survey finding.

Job to be done: “Help me understand what crypto is, what it could cost and what risks apply, so I can decide whether to explore further—or stop.”

Needs to investigate: clear vocabulary, credible risk explanations, transparent costs and understandable differences between features.

EVIDENCE DECISION

Why research this segment?

Evidence: high general awareness coexists with much lower reported ownership or usage in separately designed surveys. Platform choice and regulatory perceptions matter in published research.

Hypothesis: some crypto-aware non-users may benefit from contextual education and a clearer feature journey.

Validation: conduct moderated UK interviews and comprehension tasks, then size the segment with representative survey or internal data. Do not assume this audience wants to trade.

Methodology note: FCA, ONS and other surveys use different definitions and sampling methods; their percentages must not be presented as a single adoption trend. No age, salary, gender or segment size has been invented.

03 / Completed desk research · 24 October 2026

What the competitive research actually found.

The automated pipeline gathered official public information across Revolut, PayPal, bunq and N26. This is a completed initial desk-research phase, not a claim of validated customer demand or a comprehensive market survey.

RESEARCH OUTPUT

45 evidence records

Topic-tagged excerpts with official-source links. They are leads for analyst review, not 45 verified market conclusions.

CATEGORY POSITIONING

From separate trading destination to integrated finance feature

Revolut emphasizes exploring crypto in the same app used to manage money; bunq highlights access without a separate login. The shared message is convenience through an existing financial relationship, not a proven customer preference.

STRATEGIC TENSION

Familiarity must not be confused with safety

An established app may feel easier to navigate, but that familiarity does not reduce volatility or product risk. A credible positioning opportunity is to pair convenience with transparent costs, limitations and risk education.

03 / First-hand observation

Technical transparency meets customer comprehension.

An entry-point communication explains execution models, fees and risks across Revolut Crypto and Revolut X. The opportunity is to test whether additional plain-language guidance improves comprehension without weakening mandatory disclosures.

WHAT WAS OBSERVED

Technical comparison on entry

  • Displayed on entering the Crypto section, before attempting a trade.
  • Explains OTC execution versus an order-book model.
  • Includes counterparty roles, fee structures and execution risks.
  • Uses specialist terminology that may require further explanation.
PROPOSED RESEARCH

Test a clearer learning layer

  • Retain full mandatory terms, pricing disclosures and risk information.
  • Test an accessible summary followed by complete details, subject to compliance review.
  • Measure understanding of trading models, costs and risks.
  • Check for confusion, misleading impressions and accessibility issues.

07 / Execution plan

How research becomes a growth strategy decision.

This project combines a completed first round of public-source competitive desk research with an actionable Strategy. The three stages below distinguish work already completed from proposed customer validation.

01 / INVESTIGATE · COMPLETED DESK RESEARCH

Customer & market

Question: What promises do integrated-finance apps make about crypto, and how do they explain costs, limitations and risks?

Work done: monitored official sources from Revolut, PayPal, bunq and N26; collected 45 automatically tagged evidence records; reviewed integrated-app messaging and disclosure themes. European references are not presented as direct UK alternatives.

Output: source-linked market findings, competitor comparison and a proposed crypto-curious decision journey. These are desk-research observations, not customer interview findings.

02 / DEVELOP · STRATEGIC PROPOSAL

Positioning & messaging

Insight to explore: convenience inside a familiar finance app is a category-level message, not necessarily a unique differentiator.

Proposed direction: combine accessible crypto exploration with clear explanations of fees, restrictions and risk. Avoid implying that a familiar app makes crypto safer.

Deliverables: positioning hypothesis, message-testing territories and stage-specific education concepts for discovery, evaluation and optional onboarding. No approved Revolut messaging is claimed.

03 / VALIDATE · PROPOSED EXPERIMENT

Experiment & measure

Hypothesis: a plain-language information layer alongside full disclosures helps UK crypto-curious adults understand costs, eligibility and risks.

Design: moderated usability and comprehension sessions comparing current public explanations with a prototype, subject to legal and compliance review.

Decision criteria: primary metric is accurate comprehension; secondary measures are clarity and finding answers. Reject changes that weaken risk understanding. No research results or conversion uplift are claimed.

Automated competitive monitoring — operational

Inputs: official public pages and a curated competitor source list. Process: scheduled GitHub Actions collects snapshots, compares text changes, tags excerpts and records unavailable or cached sources. Outputs: a source-linked competitive brief, structured evidence and a coverage report.

What is not automated: validation of every excerpt, customer research and strategic decisions. AI-written synthesis is optional and not configured in the latest run; the working pipeline is rules-based automation with human interpretation.
