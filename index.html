Revolut Crypto / Hypothetical UK launch

Make learning the first step into crypto.

A focused go-to-market concept for Crypto Learn & Earn: help curious Revolut customers discover short, relevant lessons before they decide whether crypto is for them.

One productOne audienceTwo campaign touchpoints

Scenario assumption: crypto rewards are available to eligible UK customers in this proposed launch. In reality, Revolut's published UK terms state that UK customers have not been eligible for Learn & Earn crypto rewards since 8 October 2023. This is a speculative exercise, not a description of a live UK reward offer. Official terms.

01 / The opportunity

They are exploring. We should not assume they are ready to invest.

An existing Revolut customer opens Crypto, explores asset information and leaves without buying. That behaviour may signal curiosity, not an intention to trade. Learn & Earn could offer a relevant next step: short lessons that help customers understand a cryptoasset, with an optional reward where eligible.

AUDIENCE

Curious existing customers

Eligible Revolut customers who viewed crypto information more than once but have not purchased. A testable audience, not a validated segment.

CURRENT ALTERNATIVE

Leave or research elsewhere

Customers can do nothing, return later or learn through external sources. We are competing for attention and understanding, not necessarily for an immediate purchase.

MARKETING OPPORTUNITY

Connect curiosity to learning

Test whether a timely explanation of an existing educational product drives meaningful lesson discovery and understanding.

02 / Product positioning

Lead with learning. Be precise about rewards.

The core value is an accessible way to understand specific cryptoassets inside an app the customer already uses. A reward may encourage discovery, but it must not be framed as guaranteed income or evidence that investing is safe.

FOR

Crypto-curious customers

People interested enough to explore, but who may prefer to understand an asset before making any decision.

VALUE PROPOSITION

Short lessons, clear explanations

Discover how selected cryptoassets work and test your understanding. Under this hypothetical UK offer, eligible lessons may include a limited crypto reward.

REASON TO BELIEVE

Existing Revolut Learn experience

Crypto Learn already provides in-app courses. This proposal changes discovery and messaging, not the underlying educational product.

Positioning principle

“Learn about crypto before deciding what to do next.” Avoid “Earn money just by learning,” which overstates the reward and shifts attention away from understanding. Rewards are cryptoassets, so their value can fall.

03 / Targeted go-to-market campaign

Two touchpoints. One clear message.

A customer has repeatedly explored Crypto without purchasing. Rather than reproducing Revolut's Bitcoin screen, these mockups show the proposed communications that could appear after the customer leaves that experience. Timing, consent, eligibility and promotion compliance require validation.

04 / Test and decision

Measure understanding, not just clicks.

Run an eligible-audience holdout test before scaling. Compare the contextual Learn card with the existing experience, then assess whether customers find the education useful.

PRIMARY SIGNAL

Qualified learning

Incremental course starts and completions, alongside quiz performance and voluntary engagement.

CUSTOMER QUALITY

Relevance and comprehension

Survey usefulness and understanding of material risks; monitor dismissals, complaints and mistaken expectations about rewards.

GO / NO-GO

Scale only with evidence

Proceed if engagement and understanding improve without misleading expectations or adverse customer signals. Review with Product, Analytics and Compliance.

This independent concept uses no Revolut internal analytics or customer interviews. Segment size, message performance and impact are unverified. Supporting background: consumer research, competitive context, product observations.
