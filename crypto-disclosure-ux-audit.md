# Revolut Crypto — Discovery-stage Disclosure Audit

**Independent product marketing case study | United Kingdom focus | 23 October 2026**  
**Status:** Observed product evidence + unvalidated research hypotheses. Not affiliated with Revolut.

## Executive summary

A first-hand Revolut app session showed a **“Reminder about our crypto services”** notice immediately upon entering the Crypto section, **before the customer tried to buy, sell, or select an asset**. The notice compares two execution channels, Revolut app and Revolut X, covering execution model, principal vs. facilitator role, fees, instruments and settlement, and limitations/risk.

**PMM opportunity to investigate:** At the *Discover* stage, could a plain-language explanation improve understanding of the essential differences and risks, while fully preserving all mandatory regulatory content?

This is a research opportunity—not proof the existing notice is ineffective or unnecessary.

## Product marketing assessment

### What works

- Presents consequential execution, pricing, fee and risk distinctions.
- Makes the separation between the consumer app and advanced exchange explicit.
- Exposes the fact that neither route has identical trade mechanics or risks.

### What should be tested

- **Audience relevance:** visitors exploring crypto may not understand why advanced exchange terminology matters immediately.
- **Information hierarchy:** many similarly weighted blocks make it hard to pick out the practical difference.
- **Plain language:** terms such as *counterparty*, *order book*, *slippage*, and *maker/taker* are not unpacked for novices.
- **Disclosure comprehension:** lengthy text is not necessarily understood, but shortening it could also be harmful. Measure comprehension directly.

## Key research questions

1. What proportion of eligible users encounter this notice at entry, and how often?
2. Is this placement prescribed by regulation, internal policy, account migration, or another event?
3. Which details can novices accurately explain after reading the current notice?
4. Would an introductory explanation plus the complete disclosure improve comprehension?
5. What differs between crypto-curious, occasional investor and advanced-trader needs?

## Illustrative copy concept — for testing only

> **Two ways to trade crypto with Revolut**  
> The Revolut app and Revolut X use different methods to execute crypto trades. They can have different fees, prices and execution risks.
>
> **Revolut app:** Revolut quotes a price and acts as your counterparty. The accepted quote is binding; the quote can differ from prices elsewhere. Retail fees apply.  
> **Revolut X:** Orders interact with a live order book. The execution price may change, and orders can fill partially or remain unfilled. Maker/taker fees apply.  
> **Crypto carries a high risk of loss.** Make sure you understand the applicable fees, execution terms and risks.  
> **Read the full execution models, fees, limitations and risk disclosures below.**

**Important:** This concept is **not** approved product copy or a replacement for the existing disclosure. An actual design must be reviewed by Legal, Compliance and UX/accessibility; all mandatory information and sequencing must remain compliant.

## Test plan

**Phase 1 — usability research:** Interview a small, diverse sample of eligible users with varying crypto knowledge. Ask them to explain the two execution models, counterparties, pricing, fees and material risks without prompting. Identify unclear terms and misleading inferences.

**Phase 2 — experimental validation (subject to approval):**

- **Control:** existing experience.
- **Variant:** short balanced orientation + unchanged complete notice, subject to review.
- **Primary KPI:** proportion of users meeting a pre-registered comprehension threshold across execution models, pricing, fees, investment loss and settlement/execution risks.
- **Secondary:** self-rated clarity, ability to locate fee details, moderated task success and support needs.
- **Guardrails:** no reduction in awareness of loss risk, unprotected investment exposure, price variance or mandatory protections; monitor complaint and confusion signals.
- **Decision:** progress only if comprehension improves and guardrails remain acceptable. No invented sample sizes or performance uplift.

## Relationship to the wider portfolio

- **Customer research:** suggests interview questions, not validated personas.
- **Customer journey:** concrete observed touchpoint at **Discover**.
- **Positioning & messaging:** clarifies the difference between accessible consumer finance experience and advanced trading, without conflating product simplicity with investment safety.
- **Competitive intelligence:** compare clarity and timing of disclosure against PayPal (UK caveat), bunq and N26 (European benchmarks), not solely crypto exchanges.
- **Future AI PMM agent:** ingest approved public product disclosures, label source/date/geography, surface complex terminology and generate *reviewable* plain-language drafts with mandatory human sign-off. Never treat a screenshot alone as complete regulatory guidance.

## Limitations and ethics

Single observed experience; no internal Revolut customer data, placement logic, regulatory review, experiment results or measured impact. Do not infer that this is the universal default, a breach of compliance, or demonstrably harmful. Respect confidential and personally identifying information in screenshots.
