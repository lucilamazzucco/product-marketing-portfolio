# UK Crypto Consumer Research & Evidence-Based Proto-Persona

**Independent desk research | 24 October 2026 | UK adults**  
**Research type:** secondary-source synthesis, not interviews with Revolut customers. **Use:** hypothesis formation for the Crypto Discovery Journey.

## Sources and methodology
1. [FCA Cryptoassets consumer research 2025](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/research-notes/cryptoassets-consumer-research-2025) — YouGov fieldwork 5 Aug–2 Sep 2025, main sample n=2,353 and a non-quota boost n=1,053 of current/former owners. Figures apply to the stated populations; the boost is not nationally representative on its own.
2. [FCA Cryptoassets consumer research 2024](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/fca-research/research-note-cryptoassets-consumer-research-2024) — a different wave; do not conflate with 2025.
3. [ONS Public opinions and social trends, November 2025](https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/wellbeing/bulletins/publicopinionsandsocialtrendsgreatbritain/november2025) — Great Britain respondents and a different definition/measurement approach.
4. [FCA crypto regulation and consumer decision-making experiment](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/research-notes/research-note-cryptoasset-regulation-consumer-decision-making-evidence-online-experiment) — hypothetical online experiment on regulatory information and beliefs.
5. [HMRC / Ipsos qualitative crypto investor research](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/research-with-cryptoasset-investors-and-industry-participants/qualitative-research-with-cryptoasset-investors-and-industry-participants-enhancing-hmrcs-insight-into-to-the-cryptoassets-industry-and-investors-i) — 53 qualitative interviews conducted Oct 2024–Jan 2025, not generalisable.
6. [BlackRock People & Money UK crypto 2025](https://www.blackrock.com/uk/literature/publication/people-and-money-crypto-uk-2025.pdf) — investor-focused industry survey; different definitions from FCA/ONS.

## Evidence summary — what the research actually says
| Published observation | Population and year | Implication for PMM | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **91%** awareness of cryptoassets | FCA / UK general public, 2025 | Awareness alone is unlikely to be the full adoption challenge | Does not measure purchase intent or Revolut awareness |
| **73%** used a centralised exchange to obtain crypto | FCA / cryptoasset users, 2025 | Specialist venues are an important behavioral context, even if excluded from unverified feature-by-feature comparison | Not the share of all UK adults or of Revolut users |
| **25%** said more UK regulation would make them more likely to invest | FCA / cryptoasset users, 2025 | Trust and understanding of protections merit testing | This is stated likelihood, not actual investment or causal effect |
| **8%** ever bought/used or currently held crypto | ONS / GB adults, Nov 2025 | Actual experience is much less widespread than awareness | Different survey question and methodology from FCA |
| Centralised exchange users in qualitative interviews emphasized perceived security and accountability | HMRC / selected investor interviews, 2024–25 | Test trust, platform choice and perceived protection | Qualitative, not representative |
| FCA tested how regulatory explanations change consumer beliefs | FCA / hypothetical online experiment, 2025 | Risk and regulatory language can shape beliefs and must be tested carefully | Do not claim any particular message improved comprehension without reading detailed results |

**Important discrepancy:** FCA 2024 YouGov research reported 12% ownership, while FCA Financial Lives 2024 reported 4% holding and ONS Nov 2025 reported 8% ever bought/used/currently held. These are *not interchangeable* metrics; definitions, periods and methods differ. Do not put them on one trend chart.

## Behavioral segmentation (hypotheses, not measured FCA segments)
| Segment | Behavior-based definition | Research priority |
|---|---|---|
| **Crypto-curious, not yet active** | Aware of crypto, has questions, little/no practical experience, may already use a finance app | **Primary discovery research** |
| **Cautious occasional participant** | Has explored or held crypto, not a frequent trader, evaluates fees and risks | Secondary comparison |
| **Experienced self-directed participant** | Familiar with specialist platforms and advanced tools | Out of scope for beginner discovery concept |

These are proposed *behavioral* groups. We do **not** assign arbitrary age, salary, gender, household structure, portfolio size or estimated segment market share.

## Primary proto-persona — The Informed Explorer
**Status:** hypothesis-based proto-persona grounded in market-level research, **not** a validated Revolut customer persona.

**Context:** An eligible UK adult who already uses a finance app, has heard of crypto, but has limited hands-on experience. Whether they already use Revolut or want crypto is a recruiting criterion to verify, not a finding from the FCA.

**Job to be done:** “When I am curious about crypto, help me understand what it is, what the risks and costs are, and which options exist, so I can decide whether to explore further or stop.”

**Possible motivations to investigate:** general curiosity, learning about a new financial product, convenience of existing app, interest sparked by peers or market coverage.

**Potential barriers to investigate:** unfamiliar terminology; unclear fees and restrictions; perceived security or regulatory protection; fear of losses and scams; confusion between educational resources and product features.

**Decision criteria to test:** clarity, credibility, cost transparency, feature suitability, risk understanding and freedom not to proceed.

**Do not assume:** this person wants to buy; trusts Revolut more than exchanges; prefers in-app education; has disposable investment capital; or is suitable for crypto.

## Evidence → hypothesis → test
| External evidence | Hypothesis for Revolut | Proposed validation |
|---|---|---|
| High awareness but ownership/experience far lower in differently designed surveys | Awareness does not imply confident product understanding | Interview crypto-aware non-users and ask them to explain risks, fees and feature differences |
| Exchange use is prevalent among cryptoasset users | Platform reputation, cost and convenience may influence evaluation | Open-ended choice tasks; do not lead with Revolut as preferred |
| FCA research points to sensitivity around regulatory status | People may overinfer protection from familiar brands or regulation | Ask what protections participants believe apply; correct misconceptions |
| Revolut screenshots show Learn, Strategies, Earn and home separately | Contextual links may help orientation | Compare current verified UK journey with a neutral discovery prototype |

## Primary research plan
- **Recruit:** UK adults aged 18+, eligible for the relevant product, split between crypto-aware non-users and occasional users; include a mix of Revolut and non-Revolut users where feasible.
- **Qualitative phase:** approximately 10–15 moderated interviews for exploratory insights, not statistically representative estimates. Ask about recent real behavior, information sources, concerns, and platform choices before showing any prototype.
- **Usability phase:** task-based comparison of verified current UK experience vs conceptual flow. Avoid purchase pressure or live trading.
- **Quantitative follow-up:** size and prioritize segments using appropriately sampled survey and, if available, anonymized internal product data; sample and power to be determined.
- **Outcomes:** correct comprehension of costs, material risks and eligibility; task success; ability to identify limitations; false-confidence signals. A good outcome may be a decision *not* to engage.

## PMM conclusion
**Recommendation:** retain the crypto-curious segment as a *research-first* audience, not as a proven growth opportunity. Validate its size, needs and suitability before allocating launch resources. Position the proposed Crypto Discovery Journey as a potential way to connect **existing** learning and feature information; do not assume a product gap exists.

**Research integrity:** external market surveys cannot establish Revolut customer motivations or product analytics. All positioning, buyer-persona and conversion hypotheses remain provisional.
